=============================================

This application bundles the audio files listed below. All of them may be used
in a commercial, advertising-supported application subject to the terms stated
for each source.

Files are re-encoded for web delivery (mono, reduced bitrate, loudness
normalised) and cut down to the single gesture each one is used for. CC0 places
no restriction on modification or redistribution, so the edited copies carry the
same terms as the originals.

Cut points were chosen by measuring each recording's energy envelope, not by
ear-free guesswork: the sources run 12-24 s of continuous material, and the owl
recordings are discrete calls separated by true silence, so an arbitrary window
would have played nothing at all. Each file now holds one gesture, with a fade
out and a re-run of loudness normalisation after the cut.

Note on graveyard-night-bed.mp3: BigSoundBank has no cemetery ambience as such.
This is a 24-second excerpt of a rural evening soundscape (wind and night
insects), which is what a graveyard bed is made of in practice. It is an
excerpt, not a seam-checked loop — see the game's audio module for how it is
looped at playback.

  File              Original                Ref     Edit

  paddle.mp3        Tennis Ball: Bounce     s0584   1.64-1.94 s
  smash.mp3         Tennis Ball: Bounce     s0584   3.36-3.72 s
  floor.mp3         Tennis Ball: Bounce     s0584   10.48-10.78 s
  glass.mp3         Knock on a Glass Door#1 s0319   0.352-0.532 s
  clap.mp3          Applause #1             s2363   0.15-1.75 s
  whistle.mp3       Plastic Whistle #1      s1017   0.00-0.85 s

The three impacts come from one 37-second recording of a tennis ball bouncing
until it settles. Individual strikes were located with silence detection rather
than picked by eye — the impacts sit at 1.67 s, 3.38 s, 10.51 s and so on — and
each was cut with its own decay. They are the same ball at different energies,
which is what a paddle strike, a smash and a court bounce actually are.

glass.mp3 is one knock on a glass door. In padel the ball coming off the glass
wall is the sound that identifies the sport, and no oscillator gets near it. The
source is a knock-knock-knock — three strikes at 0.36 s, 0.54 s and 0.72 s — so
only the first is kept, cut at 0.18 s where its decay ends and before the second
begins. A single bounce has to sound like a single bounce.

clap.mp3 is "several people applauding indoors", chosen because it is applause
and nothing else. Recordings that mix in cheering, shouting or bar noise were
rejected: the crowd acknowledges the point, it does not celebrate it.

The net cue stays synthesized: a dead, muffled thud has no good CC0 recording,
and synthesis does that particular job well.


3. FULGOR - BANDA SONORA, STINGERS Y AMBIENTES
-----------------------------------------------

Status:  PENDING. No audio has been added to the repository for this game yet.

This section is the gate, not the review. FULGOR's design budgets 28 music pieces (three of
them delivered as three synchronised layers each), 5 stingers and 12 ambience beds, and the
rule agreed for it is a hard one:

  NO TRACK ENTERS THE REPOSITORY WITHOUT ITS ROW HERE.

The project is advertising-supported, which means commercial use, and that rules two licence
families out entirely:

  NC (non-commercial)  - incompatible with an ad-supported build.
  ND (no derivatives)  - incompatible with what this game does to its music. The adaptive
                         layers of the score are built by cutting, looping and separating
                         source material into stems, and every one of those is a derivative
                         work. ND would forbid the technique the soundtrack is designed around.

Admissible, in order of preference:

  Source                          Licence                 Use
  ------------------------------- ----------------------- --------------------------------
  OpenGameArt (CC0 filter)        CC0                     Game loops and stingers
  Kenney.nl                       CC0                     Interface and effects
  BigSoundBank                    CC0                     Real-world ambience
  freesound.org (CC0 filter)      CC0                     Rain, traffic, transformer hum
  Musopen / IMSLP                 Public domain           Strings and piano for scenes
  Free Music Archive (CC0/CC-BY)  CC0 / CC-BY 4.0         Themes and beds
  Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)     CC-BY 4.0               Large catalogue by mood
  Pixabay Music                   Pixabay Content Licence  Fill only, verified track by track

Two notes checked against the sources before this section was written:

  - Incompetech remains CC-BY 4.0 and permits commercial use, with a required credit line in
    the form "Title - Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) - Licensed under Creative Commons: By
    Attribution 4.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/". Any CC-BY track used
    also needs that credit inside the game, on a credits screen reachable from the menu.
  - The Pixabay Content Licence permits commercial use without attribution, but forbids
    redistributing the content AS music files. Embedded in a game that is not redistribution,
    so it is admissible - but it is last on the list and every track must be checked on its
    own page, because Pixabay hosts contributor uploads and the licence is theirs to get wrong.

Path when the files arrive: public/assets/fulgor/audio/

They live in public/ rather than being imported from the module on purpose: the game loads
only the current chapter's tracks (three files at boot, not a 26 MB manifest), and files
imported statically from a module are pulled into the bundle whole, which would defeat that.

Per-file budget: 900 KB per music loop, 250 KB per ambience, 40 KB per stinger, 26 MB total.
