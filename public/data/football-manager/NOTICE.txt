The adjacent JSON files record source URLs, retrieval dates, SHA-256 hashes,
Git commit or entity revisions, temporal cutoffs and coverage gaps.
Ratings are GameLock simulation estimates derived from prior results.
Missing results and incomplete player membership statements remain missing.

These data licenses do not apply to football club crests, competition marks,
portraits, trophy artwork or other assets already present in the application.
